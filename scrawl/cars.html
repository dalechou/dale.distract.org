2021 Hyundai Kona SE

This is a white, compact SUV with a turbocharged 2-litre 4-cylinder gas engine. We got it from CarMax in 2024 with 23,672 miles on the odometer. The man who sold it to us was an older gentleman, who spent hours making sure that we are going to be OK on our new journey.

We love this car. It is reliable. It has transported us to otherworldly places, like the Death Valley. We have spent weekends sprawling on the ground learning how to put on snow chains. I hand wash it every four weeks with wax and two buckets of water. We see California through this car. I trust this car enough to make it my little one's future first car, when she is old enough to drive.

2010 Nissan Tiida

Dad bought this car for me when we moved to New Taipei City. This is Little Blue, a small sedan with a 1.5-litre 4-cylinder that has seen Suhua Highway on my first date with wifey. We almost fell off the cliff that day.

This was a roomy vehicle, in spite of the compact size. Very much a family car. I became a much better driver out of this car. We took little one to complete her vaccination schedules in this car. We visited the northern shores and the Taiwanese mountainous terrains in this car. Wifey learnt how to drive through Little Blue. Eventually, she built enough confidence to drive to school and around the city.

When we moved to California, we had to sell Little Blue after 14 years and over 80k miles. We got less than $400. Wifey and little one cried saying goodbye.

But this was not the end of Little Blue. We got a parking citation in April 2026, two years later. Little Blue is still active in Taipei.

2001 Mitsubishi Lancer

This was my first car. A white, sedan version of Mitsubishi Lancer with a 1.8-litre 4-cylinder, which has nothing to do with the Evo. Dad got it for me in the second semester of university, before I did anything terrible to his Cefiro. As a newbie, I ruined this car. There was a column at the garage that was right next to my spot that proved to be too challenging for me. I had dents along the right rocker panel and the front panel. There was also one on the door.

The car was given to my cousin when I moved to Sydney. When I saw it again years later, it looked OK from the outside but had cigarette burns everywhere inside.

2003 Honda Jazz Sport

This car deserves an honourable mention because I damaged the poor machine so many times. This was mum's car in Bangkok. I did terrible things, and to this day my mother remembers them all.

(According to her) I drove from Isetan department store to On Nut for half an hour without releasing the emergency brake. I scraped off the rocker panel—yes, again—at the gas station against an elevated curb. I totalled the car when I worked in Surin, when I made a U-turn and smashed into another car. The car was parked in front of the owner's shop, so she saw the whole thing in horror. I ended up becoming friends with the owner.

2001 Nissan Cefiro A33

This was my dad's car. Listing it here because I kicked off university life with this car. It was a black, heavy vehicle with a 3-litre V6 engine and a comfortable trim. Dad used to let me drive it to school before driving off to work, and I remember it made me feel like a proper adult. He also taught me how to handle this large sedan on Taiwan's narrow streets.
